Part one

Five boundaries you're allowed to have

A boundary is not a punishment for them. It's a promise to you. Borrow these word-for-word until your own come naturally.

With an ex

"If they text me, I will wait 24 hours and check my reasons before replying — if I reply at all."

Re-entry into your life should require repair, not just reappearance.

With family

"I can love my family and still leave the room when the conversation turns disrespectful."

Love and limits are allowed to exist in the same sentence.

With friends

"I will not be the only one reaching out. Effort has to flow both ways."

You are allowed to notice who only calls when they need something.

At work

"I will take my breaks and my time off without apologizing for them."

Rest is part of the job of being a person.

With yourself

"I will not talk to myself in a tone I would never use with someone I love."

The longest relationship of your life deserves your kindest voice.

Five red flags, finally decoded

These are common patterns, not diagnoses of any one person. Only you know the full context of your situation — but naming a pattern is how it stops confusing you.

Love bombing

Intense attention, constant messages, and soulmate talk very early — before they truly know you.

Real love can afford to move slowly. Rushing is a signal, not a romance.

Hot and cold

All-in one week, distant the next. The unpredictability keeps you anxious and grateful for crumbs.

Consistency is the minimum, not a reward you have to earn.

Gaslighting

"That never happened." "You're imagining things." Your memory becomes the thing on trial.

Your perception is allowed to exist even when it is inconvenient for someone else.

The non-apology

"I'm sorry you feel that way." Apologies that protect their image and reassign the fault to you.

Repair sounds like changed behavior, not rehearsed words.

Guilt trips

"After all I've done for you." Your no gets punished instead of respected.

Guilt is not proof you did something wrong. Sometimes it is proof you did something new.

Before you ask

What does the Daily Companion cost?

$17, once. No subscription, no upsells inside, nothing locked behind a second price. It's yours for life, on all your devices.

How does access work after I buy?

Your purchase email arrives instantly with a private link and access code. Tap the link, and the app opens and remembers you — that's it.

Is my journal private?

Completely. Everything you write in the Daily Companion is saved only on your own device — there's no account and no server. We couldn't read it if we wanted to, and we don't want to.

Is this therapy?

No — and we'll never pretend it is. The Collective's tools offer encouragement and educational support. They're not a replacement for professional mental health, medical, legal, or emergency services. If you're in crisis or danger, please reach out to local emergency services or a licensed professional.

How do the paid tools arrive?

Instantly, through Gumroad. Each tool is a single file that opens in any browser — computer, tablet, or phone. Buy once, keep forever.
